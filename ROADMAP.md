## 🏛️ 12-Week Track: Staff+ Mastery Track

Comprehensive masterclass including low-level object-oriented design, machine learning pipelines, and all 35 case studies.

- **Weeks 1-4**: Comprehensive distributed systems foundation (Sections 01 to 08).
- **Weeks 5-7**: Cloud infrastructure, Security, Observability, and Storage (Sections 09 to 15).
- **Weeks 8-9**: Real-time systems (Section 16) and Low-Level Design (Section 17 - SOLID, GoF, and all 10+ worked OOP problems).
- **Week 10**: Data and ML systems (Section 18 - Data Lakes, Iceberg, Feature Stores, RAG architectures).
- **Weeks 11-12**: Case Studies 01 through 35, mock interview loops, and ADR creation.
